Company Policies — RAG Training Document

1. Mobile Device Policy

Employees may use company-issued mobile devices for approved business
activities. Each device must use a screen lock, current security updates, and
applications approved by the information technology department. Lost or
stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately. Personal use should remain
limited and must not interfere with work responsibilities.

2. Information Security Policy

Confidential company information must not be copied to personal cloud storage,
personal email accounts, or unapproved removable media. Employees must use
strong, unique passwords and must never share account credentials. Suspected
phishing messages, unauthorized access, or other security incidents must be
reported to the security team immediately.

3. Remote Work Policy

Remote employees must protect confidential conversations and documents from
unauthorized access. Company systems should be accessed through approved
devices and secure network connections. Employees remain responsible for
meeting working-hour, communication, performance, and information-security
requirements while working remotely.

4. Leave Policy

Planned leave should be submitted through the company leave system and approved
by the employee's manager. Unexpected absence should be reported as soon as
reasonably possible. Available leave balances and regional requirements may
vary, so employees should consult Human Resources for case-specific guidance.

5. Smoking Policy

Smoking is prohibited inside company offices, warehouses, meeting rooms, and
company vehicles. Employees must use designated outdoor smoking areas where
local rules permit smoking. This policy also applies to visitors and
contractors while they are on company premises.

6. Company Vehicle Policy

Only authorized employees may drive company vehicles. Drivers must follow local
traffic laws, wear seat belts, and report accidents, damage, or mechanical
problems promptly. Alcohol consumption and illegal substances are prohibited in
company vehicles. The policy does not state whether eating is permitted inside
company vehicles.

7. Workplace Conduct Policy

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, contractors, and visitors with
professionalism and respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and violent
behavior are prohibited. Concerns should be reported to a manager, Human
Resources, or the appropriate confidential reporting channel.

8. Data Retention Policy

Business records must be stored only in approved systems and retained according
to the applicable retention schedule. Employees must not delete records that
are subject to a legal hold, audit, or active investigation. Questions about a
record category should be directed to the legal or compliance team.

9. Incident Reporting Policy

Workplace injuries, information-security incidents, lost company equipment,
vehicle accidents, and suspected policy violations should be reported promptly
through the appropriate company channel. Emergency situations should be
reported to local emergency services before internal reporting procedures are
followed.
